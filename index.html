Aim

To separate a mixture of two miscible liquids (acetone and water) by simple distillation.

Theory

The separation of two miscible liquids having at least a difference of 25 K in their boiling points can be separated by simple distillation. Acetone (BP: ~56 °C) evaporates first and faster than water (BP: 100 °C).

Key Observations

Acetone vapour rises, is cooled in the condenser, and collects in the receiving beaker. The liquid remaining in the flask is water.
